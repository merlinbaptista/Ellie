Record in English unless the non-English phone strings shown have received native-speaker review. Use Presenter mode and `F` for the side-by-side recording layout. Keep developer panels closed and system audio enabled for Ellie’s speech.

| Time | Presenter step                                          | On screen                 | Spoken line (25 words max)                                                                                                           |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0:00 | Intro                                                   | Simulator idle            | “Home maintenance is scattered across memory, reminders, and provider apps. Ellie connects those moments to the home’s own history.” |
| 0:20 | `due-this-week`                                         | Due card                  | “Normal reminders remember a time. Ellie remembers your home: the purifier filter is overdue, and gas is due today.”                 |
| 0:40 | `two-intent-hero`                                       | Two cards                 | “One sentence records a cylinder change and diagnoses a beeping purifier using the household’s prior filter and warranty history.”   |
| 1:20 | `send-fix`, `open-fix`                                  | Phone fix page            | “The detailed steps, parts, warranty context, and repair-scoped chat move to the phone.”                                             |
| 1:50 | `day-thirty-reminder`, `open-booking`, `confirm-booked` | Reminder and booking page | “At the reminder date, Ellie sends a booking link using the selected regional provider.”                                             |
